# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the cora project to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. We pledge to
act and interact in ways that build an open, welcoming, and healthy community.

## Our standards

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as our code of conduct. In short: be respectful and
constructive, assume good faith, give and accept feedback gracefully, and
focus on what is best for the community. Harassment and other unacceptable
behavior described in the Covenant will not be tolerated.

The full text is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the repository,
issues, pull requests, and discussions) and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer
at **anthropic@imla.ch**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be
respected. Maintainers will follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines in
determining the consequences of any action they deem in violation.
